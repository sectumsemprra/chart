Thesis Proposal: NSR for Chart Reasoning
Research Question
Can Negative Sample Reinforcement (NSR) improve chart reasoning while maintaining output
diversity, especially on out-of-distribution charts?
Background
What the Papers Did
Chart-RVR Paper:
Fine-tuned Qwen2.5-VL-3B on chart reasoning using GRPO
Added verifiable rewards: chart type, table reconstruction, process conformity
Results: 84.56% ChartQA, 53.36% EvoChart (OOD)
Data: 6K samples from ChartQA/PlotQA/ChartFC with CoT rationales
NSR Paper:
Decomposed RLVR into PSR (reinforce correct) and NSR (penalize incorrect)
Key finding: NSR alone matches/beats GRPO on math reasoning
NSR maintains diversity (high Pass@k), PSR improves Pass@1 but hurts Pass@k
W-REINFORCE (λ=0.1): downweight PSR, keep full NSR → best results

W-REINFORCE (λ=0.1) balances both
Why NSR for Charts?
Chart Reasoning Needs Diversity:
1. Visual variations: Different styles/colors → need flexible interpretations
2. Multiple valid paths: Type→Table→Calculate can be done different ways
3. OOD robustness: Unseen chart styles benefit from diverse strategies
NSR Preserves This:
Suppresses wrong answers without collapsing distribution
Redistributes probability to plausible alternatives
Maintains pretrained visual understanding
